Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Overview

PMG Hub ("the App") is a private social media management tool operated by Ponsor Media Group ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use PMG Hub to manage social media accounts for your clients.

PMG Hub is a business tool used exclusively by Ponsor Media Group to manage content posting, scheduling, and social media engagement on behalf of our clients.

Information We Collect

When you connect a Facebook Page or Instagram account to PMG Hub, we collect:

  • Facebook and Instagram account access tokens
  • Facebook Page IDs and Instagram Business Account IDs
  • Page names and profile information
  • Post content, captions, and media URLs you provide for scheduling
  • Basic profile information (name, email) of the authorizing user

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Publish posts and content to connected Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts on your behalf
  • Schedule content for future publishing
  • Repost stories and tagged content as requested
  • Manage social media engagement for connected accounts

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage & Security

Access tokens and account credentials are stored securely as encrypted environment variables on Vercel's infrastructure. We do not store tokens in plain text or in any publicly accessible location.

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your data. Access to PMG Hub is restricted by password and available only to authorized Ponsor Media Group personnel.

Facebook and Instagram Data

PMG Hub uses the Meta Graph API to interact with Facebook and Instagram on your behalf. Our use of data received from Meta APIs complies with the Meta Platform Policy and Meta Terms of Service.

We only request permissions that are necessary for the social media management functions described in this policy. We do not request access to personal messages, friend lists, or any data beyond what is needed to publish and manage content.

Data Retention

We retain access tokens for as long as necessary to provide social media management services. You may revoke access at any time by disconnecting the app from your Facebook or Instagram account settings, or by contacting us directly.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your data at any time
  • Revoke app access through Facebook's App Settings
  • Request a copy of any data we hold about your account

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@justinponsor.com.

Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, visit our Data Deletion page or contact us at info@justinponsor.com. We will process all deletion requests within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify affected users of any material changes. Continued use of PMG Hub after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
