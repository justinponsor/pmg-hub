Data Deletion Request

How to request deletion of your data from PMG Hub

Your Right to Delete

You have the right to request deletion of any personal data or account information that PMG Hub has collected through your Facebook or Instagram account connection. We will process all requests within 30 days.

Option 1 — Revoke Access Directly

The fastest way to remove PMG Hub's access to your accounts:

1

Go to Facebook Settings

Visit facebook.com/settings → Apps and Websites

2

Find PMG Hub

Look for "PMG Hub" in your connected apps list

3

Remove Access

Click "Remove" to revoke all access. This immediately invalidates all access tokens.

Option 2 — Email Us

To request complete deletion of all data associated with your account, contact us directly. Include your Facebook Page name or Instagram handle so we can locate your data.

Send Deletion Request

What Gets Deleted

Upon a deletion request we will remove:

  • All access tokens associated with your account
  • Your Page IDs and Instagram account identifiers
  • Any scheduled posts not yet published
  • All profile data collected during authorization

Note: Content already published to your social media accounts remains on those platforms and must be deleted directly through Facebook or Instagram.

Confirmation

After processing your request, we will send a confirmation email to the address associated with your Facebook account within 30 days. If you have questions, contact us at info@justinponsor.com.
